Fair questions

Things people ask before their first order

Is this allowed? Some companies have gift policies.

Most gift policies set a dollar limit, usually somewhere between $25 and $100 per person. A box of cookies split across an office sits well under that. If a prospect works somewhere strict, tell us and we skip them.

What if nobody books?

You still get a delivery photo and a warm name to call. Most of our meetings come from the follow up call placed the day after the box lands, not from the scan itself. We tell you exactly when to call.

Where does 38% come from?

It is our booking rate across the orders we have run so far, measured as boxes delivered against meetings that actually appeared on a calendar. It is a young number on a small sample and your list, your offer and your follow up will move it in either direction.

Do you need the decision maker's name?

Helps a lot, but no. A box addressed to the office with the right job title on the card gets walked to the right desk more often than you would think.

How fast?
