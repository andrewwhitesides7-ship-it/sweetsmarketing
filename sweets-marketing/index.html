What counts as a meeting?

A scheduled phone call or video meeting with someone at one of your targeted companies, booked within 30 days of delivery. Whether they book directly through your QR link or on a follow-up call, it counts.

What is the minimum order size?

Our minimum order is $300, which covers 5 boxes at $60 per box. This gives us enough volume to back our 2-meeting guarantee.

How does the 2-meeting guarantee work?

If fewer than 2 meetings are booked within 30 days of your 5 boxes landing, we send another 5 boxes to 5 new prospects completely free of charge.

Is this allowed by corporate gift policies?

Yes! Corporate gift policies usually limit individual gifts to $25–$100. A $60 cookie box shared across an office team easily falls within standard policy limits.

We partner with local bakeries nationwide. Your 5 boxes can go to 5 different offices in 5 different states on the same day.
