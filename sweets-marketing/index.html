Fair questions

Things people ask before their first order

What exactly counts as a meeting?

A scheduled call or in person meeting with someone at one of the five companies, booked within 30 days of the last delivery. Booked through your link or booked on a follow up call, either counts. If they book and then no show, that still counts on our side, but tell us and we will usually send another box anyway.

Is this allowed? Some companies have gift policies.

Most gift policies set a dollar limit, usually somewhere between $25 and $100 per person. A box of cookies split across an office sits well under that. If a prospect works somewhere strict, tell us and we swap them for someone else at no charge.

How do I know you will honor the guarantee?

Because half your money is still in your pocket. You pay $150 up front and the second $150 only after your second meeting is on the calendar. If we miss, you never pay it, and you still get five more boxes. That is the whole mechanism, there is no claim form.

Do you need the decision maker's name?

Helps a lot, but no. A box addressed to the office with the right job title on the card gets walked to the right desk more often than you would think.

What if my prospects are not all in one city?

Fine. We source from a bakery near each office, so five boxes can go to five different states on the same day.
