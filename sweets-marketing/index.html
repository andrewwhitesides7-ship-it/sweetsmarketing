How it works

What we send

The research is the product. The box is the escalation.

  • The named decision maker, not "office manager." The person who can actually approve a quote.
  • The X-date where it exists, and a coverage trigger where it doesn't. New DOT authority, new contractor license, new building, new headcount.
  • A card written for that business specifically, carrying your agency's name and your producer's booking link.
  • Sealed bakery packaging, because a gift from a stranger has to be safe to accept before it can work.

Not every account gets a box. The ones with the nearest renewal and the best fit do. That is what keeps the merchandise value inside Utah's promotional item rules, and it is what keeps the whole thing worth doing.

What counts as a meeting

Defined before the first box ships, because this is where pay-per-meeting deals go wrong.

  • Booked and held, not just booked
  • 20 minutes minimum
  • With an owner or someone who can approve a quote
  • No-shows do not count
  • A reschedule counts once, not twice

We get paid per meeting, so you should assume we would book junk if we could. We set the bar higher than you would.

What we don't promise

Questions

The ones every agency owner asks

Doesn't this violate rebating rules?

The merchandise value stays under Utah's threshold for promotional items, nothing in the box is contingent on anyone buying anything, and every account that fits your stated criteria gets the same treatment whether they quote or not. Your agency's card goes inside. Run it past your own carrier guidelines and compliance, and we will adjust the criteria to whatever they require.

What counts as a meeting?

Booked and held, 20 minutes minimum, with an owner or a person authorized to approve a quote. No-shows do not count. A reschedule counts once, not twice.

Commercial is relationship driven. Will this actually move anyone?

That is the reason it works. Everyone else emails and gets deleted. You show up on the desk 90 days early asking for nothing except permission to bid. Most owners quietly suspect they are overpaying and nobody has asked them in years.

Why three months?

A box lands 90 to 120 days before an X-date, follow-up runs two to three weeks after, and the meeting books inside that window. A one month engagement cannot complete the arc, so it would just be an expensive way to find out nothing.

What do you need from us?

Your ICP and target account criteria. X-dates wherever you have them. Named prospects if you have a list, criteria if you don't. A booking link for each producer. Permission to send email and LinkedIn touches in your name. And any accounts you want excluded.

What is this replacing?

Usually a producer on a $50K draw with a 12 to 18 month ramp and a real chance of washing out, or you prospecting on your own nights and weekends. Compare it to that, not to nothing.
